# Contributing work records

Use the Issue templates to report bounded work, bugs, incidents, features,
initiatives, operations, or time-boxed investigations. Keep the record focused
on the intended outcome, verification, and relevant public evidence.

Do not include secrets, credentials, personal data, payment data, private media,
or security-sensitive implementation details. Maintainers will apply the
organisation-specific work type, priority, and domain during triage.

For work that belongs to a specific product or repository, use that repository.
Cross-repository or organisational coordination is maintained in a private
CLABS repository and will be linked by the appropriate maintainers.
